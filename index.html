Important Notice & Non-Liability

EuroInference is an open-source, non-commercial informational tool designed for comparative benchmarking. Model identifiers, specifications, and listed rates are automatically aggregated and normalized from public third-party provider APIs and official European Central Bank (ECB) reference rates. All pricing figures represent net estimates excluding statutory Value Added Tax (VAT) and provider-specific billing surcharges. Converted figures reflect periodic ECB reference benchmarks rather than real-time transactional spot rates. While all information is maintained with reasonable care, data is provided on an 'as is' basis without representations, warranties, or liability beyond mandatory statutory limits. All product names, logos, and brands belong to their respective owners and do not constitute an endorsement or affiliation. Users must independently verify binding service level agreements, live rate sheets, token accounting methods, and quotas directly with each respective provider before deployment. Price comparisons do not assess overall suitability. Cache-adjusted workload figures depend on user-selected assumptions (cached input share, reuse rounds) and published provider cache rates; offers without published rates are estimated at full input price. All formulas, fallback rules and calibration are documented in the Cost & Caching Methodology. Verify provider features, limits, latency, reliability, privacy, security, and service terms before deployment.
